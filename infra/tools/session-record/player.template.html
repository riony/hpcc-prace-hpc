    • Replay on the clusterThe typed commands are rebuilt from the keystrokes and typed again in a fresh login as the same user. Job IDs map in submission order (__MAP__).
    • Independent of the userSlurm's accounting is read in the controller pod, where no user has a shell. Here a finding stands on T1, and the user's recording is the context around it. The dashboard's Scenarios tab shows the same run after its reviewed evidence is merged and deployed.
